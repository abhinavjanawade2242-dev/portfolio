02 // THE NARRATIVE JOURNEY

How I Learned to Build Machines That Reason

A personal walk-through from mastering foundational sciences in Belagavi to engineering autonomous RAG agents and computer vision models in Mangalore.

2022 — 2024

The Foundation in Exact Sciences

Before training deep neural networks, I honed a strict passion for mathematical rigor and algorithmic deduction during my Pre-University studies at Mahesh PU College, Belagavi, graduating with a 91.33% distinction. Understanding linear algebra, statistics, and calculus gave me an intuitive comprehension of gradient descent and high-dimensional vector spaces.

2024 — 2025

Immersion in Data Science & Relational Architecture

Entering Alva's Institute of Engineering and Technology (AIET) for my Bachelor of Computer Science and Engineering (Data Science), I dove into Python, C, relational modeling, and algorithmic complexity. Maintaining an 8.5 CGPA, I built database-driven platforms like the Student Attendance Management System and relational backend architectures.

2026 (EARLY)

The RAG & LLM Revolution

As Large Language Models emerged, I recognized their greatest pitfall: hallucination. In summer 2026, I engineered EduAI — a complete Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) assistant for schools that anchors LLM answers strictly to uploaded curricula. Later, with the Wikimedia AI Research Assistant, I scaled vector retrieval across 25,000+ chunks with automated evidence verification.

CORE INSIGHT

"An AI system is only as reliable as its citation grounding. Semantic search paired with deterministic fact-checking transforms an LLM from a toy into an enterprise tool."

2026 (LATE)

Solving Real-World Human Problems

I turned to Computer Vision and Healthcare. Developing the Multi-Crop Disease Diagnosis System for coastal Karnataka farmers (paddy, coconut, arecanut) using Grad-CAM heatmaps proved that deep learning must be explainable. Soon after, I engineered ReportSaathi to translate laboratory medical reports into simple English, Hindi, and Kannada.

HACKATHONS & RECOGNITION

Won honors at Technovate 2.0 and Innovexa 1.0 Hackathons, tackled the Amazon ML Challenge for Business Entity Resolution, and continuously deploy code that makes technology accessible to everyone.

THE HORIZON

What Lies Ahead

I am eager to contribute to forward-thinking engineering teams, high-throughput data platforms, and applied AI labs. Whether building agentic workflows, scalable data pipelines, or fine-tuning neural architectures, I bring technical curiosity, speed, and disciplined execution.
